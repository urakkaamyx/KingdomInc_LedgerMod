[b][size=5]Ledger Mod[/size][/b]
See what your production chains produce, what they consume, and whether they earn their keep.

Place Ledger between the last producer and its destination to track passing items, upstream resources, operating costs and profit or loss. Item counts and a compact currency report stay visible on the node, with a detailed breakdown available from the gear button.

[b][size=4]Features[/size][/b]
[list]
[*][b]Live item tracking.[/b] Item names, native icons and quantities stay visible on the node. The list expands as additional item types pass through.
[*][b]Profit and loss by currency.[/b] A compact two-column display shows one combined net value for each affected currency. Gains are green and losses are red. Unused currencies stay hidden.
[*][b]Gold, silver and copper.[/b] Gold amounts use the game's familiar coin denominations. Requisition, Medals, Rings, Mana, Crowns, Ducats, Ingenuity and Faith retain their own units and icons.
[*][b]Second, minute or hour reports.[/b] Choose a rolling reporting window from the gear menu. A slim gold progress bar shows the selected interval filling and repeating.
[*][b]Upstream production breakdown.[/b] Inspect observed production stages, raw resources and operating spend, including recruitment and equipment branches through Outfitter. Routing nodes and empty placeholder rows stay out of the report.
[*][b]Actual destination income.[/b] Track rewards from Item Shops, Luxury Shops, Requisition Offices, Disenchanters, Restaurants and Mercenary Guilds, including destinations reached through native storage and routing nodes.
[*][b]One to five matching lanes.[/b] Start with one input and one output, then add matching pairs through the gear menu. Each lane operates independently.
[*][b]Zero item storage.[/b] Ledger forwards items directly. If its receiver is full, disconnected or rejects the item, that item stays at the producer. Full Stockpiles, shop tills and Requisition Offices also block admission.
[*][b]Native game styling.[/b] Bronze frames, burgundy panels, the game's pixel font and native item/currency icons keep the report consistent with Kingdom Inc.
[/list]

[b][size=4]How the accounting works[/size][/b]
For each affected currency:
[b]Profit / loss = recorded income minus observed operating costs.[/b]

Currencies are kept separate. A chain that spends Gold and earns Requisition shows its Gold loss and Requisition gain independently; Ledger does not invent an exchange rate or assign a guessed price to raw resources.

Production costs and upstream crafting currency rewards follow the items that pass through Ledger. Destination income is recorded when the supported building earns it, including rewards added to its collection tray. Collecting that tray manually or automatically does not count the same income again.

Requisition Offices retain unfinished contract contributions until a reward is issued. Shared contributions divide that real reward proportionally; pre-existing progress and passive generation keep their own uncredited share. Disenchanter passive Mana is excluded too.

Paid chains can show operating losses without a shop. Chains with no currency activity remain useful as item-throughput reports. The timer bar repeats independently of the rolling report; it does not wipe the totals when it reaches the end.

[b][size=4]Getting started[/size][/b]
1. Research Accountant, then research Ledger.
2. Place Ledger from the Utility build menu, between the last producer and its destination.
3. Connect an input and its matching output.
4. Open the gear to select Second, Minute or Hour and add up to five matching pairs.
5. Let newly produced items pass through, then inspect Items passed and Chain costs.

[b][size=4]Requirements and installation[/size][/b]
Requires Kingdom Inc's Unity Mono build and [b]BepInEx 5[/b]. No other mod is required.

1. Install BepInEx 5 for the game if it is not already installed.
2. Close the game.
3. Extract the player ZIP into the Kingdom Inc folder containing KingdomInc.exe.
4. Confirm the DLL is at [b]BepInEx/plugins/LedgerMod/LedgerMod.dll[/b].
5. Launch the game.

To update, close the game and replace the existing LedgerMod.dll with the new version. Input/output-pair and reporting-window settings are saved with the node.

[b][size=4]History and compatibility[/size][/b]
[list]
[*]Accounting history is session-local and resets when a kingdom is loaded. Allow fresh production to pass through after loading.
[*]Items already in storage or production may have missing upstream history. The node marks the known net amount with [b]~[/b]; the detailed report withholds a complete net figure while affected history remains in the selected window.
[*]Production costs are attributed when items pass Ledger. This is an item-based chain report, not a log of every change to the kingdom's wallet. Building purchases and research costs are excluded.
[*]Native resource, Forest, crafting and Outfitter production are supported. Special training/combat chains, portals that reconstruct items and other mods' transformations may have incomplete cost history.
[*]Party-Up's whole-party output requires a receiver with storage capacity, so Ledger cannot serve as its direct whole-party receiver. Ordinary production pipes are supported.
[*]Manual installation is supported. Vortex deployment has not been verified.
[/list]

[b][size=4]Uninstalling[/size][/b]
Remove Ledger nodes from every kingdom you intend to keep, reconnect their production chains, save, and close the game. Then remove the LedgerMod plugin folder. Do not load a save containing Ledger nodes after removing the mod.

[b][size=4]Bug reports[/size][/b]
Include your game version, Ledger version, other installed mods, a screenshot of the connected chain and the relevant section of [b]BepInEx/LogOutput.log[/b]. For missing costs or income, describe which items pass through and which building receives them.
